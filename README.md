## Areas I work on

- **Enterprise AI and decision intelligence** — moving beyond chat interfaces to systems that connect context, evidence, analysis, and action.
- **AI agents and Claude skills** — designing useful workflows with clear responsibilities, tool access, and human checkpoints.
- **MCP and enterprise integration** — exploring how AI can work with business systems through explicit, governed interfaces.
- **Salesforce and enterprise architecture** — applying platform experience to practical workflows, data, integration, and transformation.
- **Knowledge, memory, and writing systems** — making information useful without losing traceability or editorial control.

## Principles behind the projects

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Evidence over confident guesses** | Make sources, assumptions, and missing information visible. |
| **Useful over theatrical** | Solve a concrete problem before adding complexity. |
| **Human control by design** | Let people review consequential edits and actions. |
| **Observable and testable** | Make behavior, results, and failure modes easier to inspect. |
| **Built to be reused** | Clear documentation, runnable examples, and practical setup matter. |

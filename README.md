## The work

I work at the intersection of enterprise technology, architecture, and business transformation. I’m interested in what it takes to move AI beyond isolated copilots and demos into systems that understand context, connect evidence, support decisions, and operate with appropriate governance.

My current areas of focus:

## Principles I build around

> Enterprise AI should do more than generate answers. It should understand the business context, show its evidence, make uncertainty visible, and keep consequential actions under the right controls.

- **Evidence over confident guesses.** Distinguish facts, calculations, inferences, and hypotheses.
- **Context before orchestration.** Agents are only as useful as the data, memory, and business semantics they can access.
- **Governance by design.** Permissions, traceability, and human approval are part of the architecture—not an afterthought.
- **Outcomes over demos.** Build for real workflows, measurable value, and maintainability.
